# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or force made a decision necessary? What constraints applied?
Keep it to the facts a future reader needs to understand the choice.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a single clear position.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or off-limits as a result. Include the trade-offs
that were accepted, not just the upside.
