### CM1 — Account liquidity role (Jordan, 12:58)

- New per-account field `liquidity_role` ∈ {`free_cash`, `credit_line`, `reserve`}. **Type beats sign.**
  - `free_cash`: counts as deployable liquidity only when balance ≥ 0.
  - `credit_line` (Lombard/Dispo/Margin): NEVER counts as free liquidity even when positive; negative = drawn credit → liability, reduces net worth; unused headroom is not liquidity (optionally shown separately as "available leverage", out of the cash quote).
  - `reserve` (earmarked, e.g. BTC autumn reserve): out of depot liquidity, shown as a labelled overlay.
- One-line rule: an account is credit/non-free if EITHER balance < 0 OR type is `credit_line` — type wins.

### CM3 — Concentration thresholds (Jordan, 12:58)

- Default + per-call override, **instrument-type-aware** (flat threshold rejected — both blind spots and false alarms).
- Defaults on the STEERABLE basis (excl. crypto/Family/Guest/Zock):
  - Single stock: WARN > 7%, HARD > 10%.
  - Broad/diversified ETF (e.g. VWCE): exempt from single-name rule or own high threshold (~25%+) — a World ETF at 20% is target, not risk.
  - Category/theme (strategy leaf): flag when over-target > 3pp OR > 150% of target weight.
  - Asset-class cap: configurable.
- All thresholds shipped as defaults, overridable per call.

### #344 rounding policy decided + ADR-0016 (Andi, 20:11)

- Andi's principle: compute/store as precisely as possible, round only for human display. Matches best practice.
- **ADR-0016** (`docs/decisions/0016-rounding-policy.md`, Accepted, indexed): full precision in all intermediate arithmetic (generalises ADR-0007); persist at column scale 6, `:half_up`; JSON API/MCP return full-precision strings (no rounding); round only at the human UI (money/percent 2dp, FX 6dp, qty ≤6dp, `:half_up`); invariants checked at full precision; largest-remainder for displayed breakdowns.
