through a summarizing fetch tool, so counts (posts, views, likes, reactions) are

errors. German posts are paraphrased in English and are not verbatim. I left out

## Praise and complaints per product

### Portfolio Performance
- Praise, all of it thin:
  - Users value the program and its importers, and fear losing them (#4, #5).
  - STALE: the mobile app was welcomed in 2024, and users paid for Premium to support the project (#23).
  - Indirect: developers build web front ends and MCP servers on PP's XML data instead of replacing its model. That is use, not a statement of praise (#17, #19).
- Complaints:
  1. Price data keeps breaking: providers stop updating, and scraping gets blocked (#7).
  2. Broker import is fragile and hard to maintain: Trade Republic CSV and DEGIRO threads, and the maintainer of the PDF importers left in June 2026 citing merge practice (#1–#4, #9).
  3. Bookkeeping edge cases (spin-offs, splits, gifted shares) are hard, and the figures are hard to understand (invested capital, the new risk line) (#11, #12).
  - STALE, 2024: the mobile app is read-only and updated prices slowly (#22, #23).

### Ghostfolio
- Praise: I found none this run.
- Complaints:
  1. Data-provider failures (#24, #26).
  2. Doubts about whether the performance calculation is correct, and total return is missing (#25).
  3. Crashes and robustness (#26).
- Caveat: the GitHub signal is thin (#27).

### Wealthfolio
- Praise:
  1. A polished UI.
  2. Local, open source, no subscription.
  3. Many platforms, and plugins (#33).
- Complaints:
  1. No automated sync (#34, #30).
  2. Cash-balance and transfer figures are wrong, and releases have broken them (#28, #29).
  3. CSV-import trouble and platform crashes (#35, #31).

### Parqet, getquin, Finanzfluss Copilot
- I retrieved no user voice this run. The only Parqet results were the vendor's own blog, which is marketing, and I did not use them.

## What switchers say they miss
- **I found no first-person statement from the last 12 months by anyone switching from PP to Parqet, getquin, Ghostfolio, Wealthfolio or Finanzfluss Copilot, or back.** Reddit, the likeliest venue, was blocked for the search tool.
- Indirect evidence of what someone leaving PP would miss or would check first:
  - **Automated import of broker documents.** PP users' replies show they fear manual entry more than anything else if the importers decay (#4). The importer base is large (#2).
  - **Correct valuations across all transaction types.** That was the first thing testers checked when they tried an alternative built on their PP data (#20).
  - **Starting from existing holdings rather than the full history** is a live onboarding question (#10).
  - **Using an LLM on existing data.** PP users are wiring LLMs to their PP XML themselves, through reports and community MCP servers, instead of switching tools. A security critique of one server (a 0.0.0.0 default) came up at once (#16–#18).
  - **Adjacent evidence from HN (budgeting apps, not trackers).** People switched because a subscription model changed or they feared a vendor shutdown. Automated sync is table stakes for many (#34, #36).
