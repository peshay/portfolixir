## Scope

The sunburst is the one chart rendering with no "Daten als Tabelle" disclosure (UX-DR10 makes it mandatory under every rendering), it carries a tutorial sentence beneath it ("Tippe auf ein Segment oder fahre mit der Maus darüber für Details."), and the plan's "Σ Soll oberste Ebene: 100,0 %" floats above it as a sentence. Target: the disclosure with its one-line purpose (the drift table beneath *is* that table and gains the disclosure head), the sentence deleted, the Σ line folded into the chart's basis line (plan · Σ Soll · view · as-of), and the empty centre carrying the touched segment — name, value, actual against target — which is what a touch device has instead of a hover tooltip. The legend shows value next to the share.
