## Scope

The Views page is the app's most prose-heavy surface: a two-paragraph "So funktioniert's — in zwei Schritten" block under the top bar (the `div.hint[data-role="how-it-works"]` the UX-DR11 inventory marked "triage required"), an explanatory sentence under each of the two section headings, two inline forms above two lists, bucket rows without swatch or usage, view rows without their rule, and a third section that duplicates what Accounts & depots shows. Target (UX-DR1 pattern, list first): each view's row says what it does ("Alles außer Crypto") and what it covers; each bucket's row carries its colour and where it is used; `+` opens each form; the tutorial becomes one ⓘ per heading; the default-bucket section becomes the usage column of the bucket rows, edited where it applies (the account row on Accounts & depots).
