## Scope

The top-bar `h1` clips the diaeresis of a capital umlaut on a 1× display: the Overview title renders as "Ubersicht" in both themes and in the committed docs screenshots. Cause: `.topbar-page h1 { overflow: hidden; line-height: 1.15 }` (`priv/static/app.css:575-583`) — at 3× the glyph is intact, so it is the line box, not the font. Fix per the DESIGN.md amendment: `line-height: 1.3` on the heading, the ellipsis clip moves to the `.topbar-page` container as `overflow-x: clip`. Re-take the docs screenshots that carry the clipped title.
