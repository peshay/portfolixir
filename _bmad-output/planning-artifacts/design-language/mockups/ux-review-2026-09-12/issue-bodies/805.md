## Scope

Each category row of a classification tree strings seven unlabelled figures together — "5 5 22.480,60 21.863,00 +617,60 2,8 % +1 ohne Bestand" — and empty categories print "0 0 0,00"; on the phone the row wraps into a number salad. Above the tree an accent-bordered banner in second person ("zieh es unten aus …") and a "Tipp: Klicke Zeilen an …" sentence. Two variants for the rows; **the owner picks** (recommended first). In both the banner becomes a `{components.data-note}` with the link, the "Tipp" is deleted (selection + toolbar are self-explanatory; drag is the accelerator), and the result's basis ("heutige Zusammensetzung, keine Periodenrendite", ADR-0041) becomes a basis line with ⓘ.

- **A — a column head above the tree (recommended):** Positionen · Wert · Einstand · Ergebnis, right-aligned, "—" for empty categories; "+1 ohne Bestand" as a muted suffix to the name.
- **B — micro-labels in the row:** no head; each figure carries its word as a 10 px label; empty categories say "leer". Better on the phone, noisier on the desktop.
