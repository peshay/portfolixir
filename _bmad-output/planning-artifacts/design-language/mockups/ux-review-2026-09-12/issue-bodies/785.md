## Scope

Two user-facing strings print an internal enum value. The transactions history shows `balance_adjustment` as a filter chip and in every balance row: `tx_type_label/1` has a clause for `"balance"` but the data kind is `balance_adjustment`, so the `to_string(other)` fallback fires (`lib/portfolixir_web/live/transaction_management_live.ex:1126-1128`). The securities detail overview prints the feed as `PORTFOLIO_PERFORMANCE`. Both go through gettext (DE and EN). The story also adds the meta-test the amendment asks for: every kind in `Transaction.kinds/0` has a label clause, so the fallback is unreachable.
