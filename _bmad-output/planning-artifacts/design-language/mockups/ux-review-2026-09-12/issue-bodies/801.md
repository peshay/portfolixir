## Scope

#721 made the custom range a real from/to pair. Opening it still pushes the "Performance" heading down, stacks "Von/Bis" beside the heading and puts the year select on a second line — the section reflows around a control. Two variants; **A picked by the owner on 2026-09-14** (recommended first; see Variants):

- **A — popover on the trigger (recommended):** from/to in one row, the year presets as chips, "Anwenden"; the heading and the chart do not move. Closes on Esc, returns focus to the trigger.
- **B — an inline row under the period control:** full width, no overlay; the row pushes only the chart down, never the heading or the toggles; "Benutzerdefiniert" becomes a token of the segmented group.

Both keep the UX-DR19 ISO text inputs and the existing validation (`data-role="range-error"`).
