## Scope

Every Cash-flow facet opens with a three-to-five-line paragraph carrying composition, scope, exclusions and (on Costs) method — the UX-DR11 habit the spine names as "prose is not the fallback". And the page subtitle is static: "Erhaltene Dividenden und Zinsen" stands over Realized gains, Flows and Costs (`income_live.ex:229`). Target: each facet opens with **one basis line** — currency · what is aggregated · scope — plus one ⓘ holding exclusions and method; each facet has its own subtitle.
