## Scope

Five small layout defects, one mechanical story:

1. **Two control rows sit outside the content grid.** The view switcher on Wealth and the facet control on Cash flow start at the sidebar edge (x = 221) while the section content starts at x = 248; at 390 px the wrapped "Standard-Ansicht ⓘ" touches the screen edge with no gutter. Both move inside `.workspace-section` padding (no negative margins).
2. **The import drop zone's "Datei auswählen" button** sits bottom-left, outside the centred composition of glyph, sentence and basis line. Centre it under the sentence.
3. **The Trades tab's ⓘ basis affordance** is clipped at the pane edge ("Kurs- & Währungsb…").
4. **The Wealth tab row scrolls at 390 px but shows no right-edge fade** — D6 of the 2026-08-18 amendment specifies a one-sided fade as the affordance.
5. **The securities "GEÄNDERT SEIT" family** sits on its own band with a different label treatment from "ANLAGEKLASSE" inline in the chip row; one treatment for family labels.
