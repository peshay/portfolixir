## Scope

At 1440×900 the Overview leaves sixty percent of the viewport empty; the value card states a number without a direction; the off-target rows are right-aligned text with no visual and no visible link affordance (the spine says each row is a link into Allocation). Three compositions; **the owner picks one** (recommended first):

- **A — sparkline in the value card, drift bars in the rows (recommended):** a 90-day value line in the card and the YTD change with its basis; each drift row gets a bar around zero (sign, colour and the direction word — UX-DR7) and reads as a link. Stays inside UX-DR2 as rewritten (value + change, off-target, data quality).
- **B — a four-cell KPI strip under the card** (TTWROR, cash quote, last booking, quote freshness). **Needs a decision:** UX-DR2 retired the hero on 2026-08-05 and the spine records the owner's wish for metric cards as an [ASSUMPTION]; picking B reopens that rule (review Part 5, Q1).
- **C — two columns:** the content of A with the right column as the inbox (off-target + data quality); the page fills the screen.
