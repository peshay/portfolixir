The bucket cell of a depot row holds four controls for one question — "BEIDE · Household × · + · Getrennt taggen" — and "Liquiditätsrolle" is printed in every row beside its select under a column already headed "LIQUIDITÄTSROLLE". Two variants; **A picked by the owner on 2026-09-14** (recommended first; see Variants):

- **A — chips with a scope line (recommended):** the chips stay, their scope reads as a sub-line ("gilt für Depot und Konto" / "erbt vom Depot" / "kein Bucket"), "getrennt taggen" moves into the row menu, the role label appears only in the head.
- **B — read-only chips, editing in the row dialog:** the cell shows chips without controls; "Bearbeiten" opens the existing account dialog with a bucket section (depot default, account default, separate).

Either variant keeps the API/MCP bucket assignment endpoints unchanged.
