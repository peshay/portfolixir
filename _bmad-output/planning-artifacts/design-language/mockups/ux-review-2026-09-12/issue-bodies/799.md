## Scope

At 390 px the securities list is a wide table of which only the name column is visible — price, change and asset class need a sideways scroll — and the transactions history clips its security names and month totals inside its scroller. The 2026-09-12 amendment adds **UX-DR27**: under 560 px a list of entities renders as two-line rows, not a scrolling table (matrices and dialog tables keep UX-DR15's scroller). Two variants; **the owner picks** (recommended first):

- **A — two-line rows (recommended):** name over identifiers; price and day change (securities) or amount and quantity × price (transactions) right-aligned; row states (stale, no price, unclassified with its remedy link) as words in the row; month group heads stay. Nothing scrolls sideways.
- **B — the table stays, the first column sticky, numeric columns compacted** so price and change are visible without wiping; the column picker keeps applying.
