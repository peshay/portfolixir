## Scope

Four things on the Income facet's chart and matrix, one story:

1. **The stacked bar has no legend** — dividends and interest differ by tint only (UX-DR7 forbids hue as the sole channel). Add the legend and direct labels in the segment where they fit.
2. **The year axis skips years** — the bars jump from 2023 to 2026, so time is misrepresented. Every year between first and last booking gets a slot, empty ones drawn as a baseline tick with "–".
3. **The year × month matrix is a wall of "0,00"** — zero cells render as a quiet "–" in `{colors.text-subtle}` so the non-zero cells are what the eye finds (Part 4 rule 6).
4. **Neither income chart has the UX-DR10 disclosure** — the adjacent table is not the disclosure (spine census). Both the annual chart and the per-month chart get "Daten als Tabelle"; the existing tables become the disclosure bodies.

Plus the owner's **accumulated-per-month series** (Amendment 2026-08-18, Part 4: "wanted, ships first"): if it has not shipped, it lands here as a line over the month bars inside the year drill; if it has, the story says where.
