## Scope

The Classifications index is a bullet list of three names, a "Wähle einen Klassifizierungsbaum …" sentence and a full-width outlined "Neue Klassifizierung" button. A tree has a size, an assignment state and a role (does it carry a plan?), and the index answers none of them. Target: one row per tree with category count and depth, assigned-of-total securities with the unassigned count as an attention word, the plan a tree carries (name · active · Σ Soll), a small colour bar of the top-level shares, the kebab for rename/delete, and `+` in the heading instead of the full-width button. The sentence goes (#791).
