## Scope

The Transactions page *is* its form: a six-field grid with an orphaned "Preis", a full-width 80 px notes field and the submit button, then a holdings table that duplicates Wealth → Holdings, then the history. The 2026-08-18 amendment (Part 4) fixed the vocabulary — one panel, a data table with the D2 chip row, no view switcher, the depot select says where a booking lands — but not where the panel sits, and the spine's progressive-disclosure rule says creating is "one intent away, never zero". Three variants; **C picked by the owner on 2026-09-14** (recommended first; see Variants):

- **A — the form as a disclosure panel above the history (recommended):** the page opens on the journal; "+ Buchung erfassen" opens one row of six fields, costs and note behind a link inside the panel; the depot field is labelled "Bucht auf Depot".
- **B — a compact always-open one-row form** for the daily booker; costs/note behind a link; history directly beneath.
- **C — a side drawer** (the securities detail pane's shape; a bottom sheet on the phone), reused for editing a booking.

In all variants the "Aktuelle Bestände" section leaves the page (it is Wealth → Holdings) and the history gains the amount column (#786).
