## Scope

At 390 px the securities toolbar is: search, a row of three icon buttons, three wrapped rows of chips, then the "Geändert seit" band — half the screen before the first row. Two variants; **the owner picks** (recommended first):

- **A — one swipeable chip strip (recommended):** search and tools on one line; one line of chips that scrolls sideways with the D6 right-edge fade and snap; the families ("Qualität", "Klasse", "Geändert") as small prefixes inside the strip; "Weitere" last.
- **B — chips behind "Filter (n)" as a bottom sheet:** the list starts directly under the search; the button carries the count of active chips; the sheet stacks the families (the mechanism the row menu already uses under 720 px).

UX-DR22 forbids collapsing *tab rows* into the burger; filter chips are not tabs, so both variants are within the rules. Above 560 px the D2 chip row is unchanged.
