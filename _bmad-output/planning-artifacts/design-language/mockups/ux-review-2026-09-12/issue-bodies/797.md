## Scope

Seven `.stat` cards in a 5+2 grid: two combined values wrap to three lines ("12.473,00 EUR · 25,1 %", "43.560,75 · +1.500,00 EUR"), the second row is an orphan of two, the band is repeated verbatim on the Allocation & targets tab, and at 390 px it is seven stacked cards (≈ 840 px) before any content. Three compositions are mocked up; **the owner picks one on the PR or here** (recommended first). In every variant the Allocation tab does not repeat the band — it carries a one-line summary linking back (Part 4 rule 4) — and no value wraps (rule 5: currency as a small suffix, combined figures as value + sub-line).

- **A — two tiers (recommended):** three lead figures (total, TTWROR, IRR) full size; four supporting figures at half height. A hierarchy instead of seven equals.
- **B — one strip:** seven cells in one row, swipeable on the phone with the D6 edge fade. Densest; loses the card identity.
- **C — 4+3 grid at 24 px:** the conservative fix.
