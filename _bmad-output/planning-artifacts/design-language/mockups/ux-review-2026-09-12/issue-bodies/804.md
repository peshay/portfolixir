## Scope

The securities detail pane opens on "Übersicht", which is the master-data edit form — eight inputs, a checkbox with a three-line helper, and a "Änderungen speichern" button — before any reading content; on the phone that is a full screen of fields. The spine's rule: reading is the default posture, editing one intent away. Two variants; **A picked by the owner on 2026-09-14** (recommended first; see Variants):

- **A — Übersicht becomes a reading surface (recommended):** six key figures (last price and date, day, 1Y, holding, value, unrealised with basis), a small price chart, the thesis state from the research log with a link to Research, the personal note; "Bearbeiten" in the header opens the master data in the existing dialog pattern.
- **B — the chart tab becomes the default; the form tab is renamed "Stammdaten" and moves last.** The smallest change; the checkbox helper becomes an ⓘ either way.
