## Scope

`EXPERIENCE.md` → Component Patterns → *Tax surface* has said since 2026-08-05 what this page is: a budget dashboard plus a check list — allowance utilisation as a fill level with the remaining amount and its as-of date, recorded statements as a list each carrying its consistency finding as a data note, entry forms behind a disclosure, the four prose paragraphs resolved to ⓘ / basis line / field help. The build has none of it: a bare number with an amber inline pill for "Veraltet", three paragraphs, a thirteen-field form in the sightline, the finding as an amber bordered banner without glyph or word, a second inline form for allowance orders. This story builds the specified shape; the mockup is the target, not a variant set.
