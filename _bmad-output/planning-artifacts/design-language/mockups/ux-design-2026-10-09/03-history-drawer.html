Variante A empfohlen — „+“ vor jedem Zufluss, keine Farbe; null ohne Vorzeichen

A — „+12,50“ / „-4,90“, ungefärbt
A — empfohlen

Why A. A booking's amount is a flow: its sign is a direction, not a gain. That is the contribution table's settled rule, DESIGN.md → Wealth → the contribution table: „Zu-/Abflüsse and Erträge signed and uncoloured — a flow's sign is a direction, not a gain.“ The history is the same kind of figure, so it takes the same form.

  • It closes the Accessibility-Floor gap by the text: „+“ and „-“ are the carriers, and nothing depends on hue (UX-DR7). It reads the same in forced colours and in the dark theme.
  • Colour would say the wrong thing. A deposit is not a gain, a buy is not a loss, and a fee is a cost, not a loss either. Green on every Einlage and red on every Kauf would make a month of buying read as a month of losing, beside a Wealth page whose green and red mean exactly gain and loss.
  • The house formatter already does it: Format.signed_decimal(amount, 2) prints „+“, „-“, and nothing for a figure that rounds to zero. signed_money/3 keeps deciding the direction (kind, transfer side); only the formatter changes, in the cell, the phone row and the delete dialog's box, which reads phone_amount/2.
  • The level stays a level: „Saldo gesetzt 3.250,00“ carries no „+“ — it states where the balance is, not what moved.

B — „+“ grün, „-“ rot

B follows DESIGN.md → Colors to the letter („Semantic colour applies wherever a sign exists“), as the trades facet and the Trades tab do.   • The colours mean gain and loss everywhere else. Here they would mean „in“ and „out“: every Kauf red, every Einlage green. The frame above reads as a red month — four buys and a fee — while the portfolio may have gained.
