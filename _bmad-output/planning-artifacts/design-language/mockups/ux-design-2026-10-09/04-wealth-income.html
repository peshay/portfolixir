A — empfohlen

A. "Marktwert" and "G&V" are money: Format.money, two places — "2.569,68". "Ø Kosten" and "Letzter Kurs" are prices per unit: the history's stored-figure rule R10c (DESIGN.md, the history's price column, amended 2026-10-07) — every stored digit up to four places, at least two, a price under 1 at least three significant digits — so "128,4838" stays what the Kurse tab and the history print for the same quote, and the projection's 28-digit average becomes "33,3333". "Stückzahl" keeps its stored digits, as everywhere. A text space goes before every suffix ("2.569,68 EUR"), the contribution table's sum row's form, so the cell reads as it looks.

Why A. It follows ADR-0016 §4 — round at the human boundary, money to two places — which the DESIGN.md sentence contradicts; the sentence was a side remark of the "G&V %" repair, not a decision with a reason of its own. The reason it gave, "this table is the human read of what the API serves", is kept where it matters: the API and MCP serve every digit, and the basis line already says the table is valued "zum zuletzt gespeicherten Kurs". A price is not money: rounding "128,4838" to "128,48" (B) would make Wealth disagree with the security's own Kurse tab and the history for one stored quote.

Found while drawing (fixed in this story, D-14): "G&V" is a signed figure with neither its "+" nor its colour ("60", "-65"), against DESIGN.md → Colors ("wherever a sign exists, at every level of a table") and issue 1010's rule; the story prints it signed in its sign colour, decided on the figure as displayed (#1149), in every variant — dashed below.
