B is one rule with no condition: every Gebühren, Steuern and Brutto cell names its currency. It is the simplest to build and test, and never wrong. Its cost is not width — a column is as wide as its widest cell, which A already suffixes, so the table measures 816 against A's 815 px at 390 — but noise: "EUR" or "USD" three more times on every row of every security, on rows whose Preis already names the one currency, and the cross-currency row that matters looks like all the others, so the suffix stops carrying news.

C keeps the figures bare and adds a muted line under a cross-currency row: "Preis und Brutto in USD · Gebühren und Steuern in EUR (Test-Cash)".
