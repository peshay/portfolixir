G1 — After a row action finds its security gone, the note is in view and has the focus Wertpapiere, H8.6 (#920) · WCAG 2.4.3

Retire, Edit, Merge into… or Delete on a security another writer deleted or merged away reloads the list and puts the note into #securities-action-result — at the top of the page, above the filters, while the operator is at the row. The design critic measured the note at −1142 px of the viewport at 1280 and at −2611 px at 390, and the focus on <body>: the menu item that had it went with the menu. After, as H2's A6 does for a booking gone meanwhile: the slot carries tabindex="-1", and on the vanished-row path only the server asks the page to bring it into view (below the sticky top bar, scroll-margin-top) and to focus it. A keyboard user sees the house ring; the next Tab reaches the survivor's link and the dismiss. Ordinary results land where they always did and take no focus: their control is still on the page.

Why the slot, not the list's heading. The securities page has no list heading of its own (the top bar's title is the page's), and the note is what the operator needs to read: the slot sits directly above the list, so bringing it to the top of the window also shows the first rows. The slot's live region still announces the note; the focus makes sure a screen-reader user who missed it lands on it, and that the next Tab does not start from the top of the page.

Where it applies. Every row action that finds its security gone — the menu's own path and the delete's “not found” branch — and “Cannot delete”'s way out on a security merged away while the dialog stood open (the dialog closes, its opener is gone too). When the vanished row was open in the detail pane the URL drops its id first; the note and the focus survive that patch.

G2 — The Drift ⓘ wraps on the phone Vermögen → Allokation & Ziele, the tree's head · H4 ④c · issue 1053

Under 560 px table { display: block; overflow-x: auto; white-space: nowrap } (the phone block of app.css) is inherited by everything inside a table, and the tree's Drift ⓘ sits in the table's head: its sentence stands on one line and runs out of its 18 rem box and off the screen. The design critic measured a scrollWidth of 2196 px in a 288 px box with ④c's second sentence; the seeded demo, whose plan is above 100 %, gives 1131 px with the first sentence alone. After: the tooltip's paragraph sets white-space: normal itself, at every width, so no table rule reaches it. Both frames are drawn at a phone's width with the phone rule applied.

G5 — The history's hover reaches the pinned Balance Transaktionen → Historie, filtered to one account · H4 ①d

With an account filter the history shows the running Balance, pinned (.col-subject, opaque so the figures scroll beneath it). DESIGN.md's H4 amendment kept this as the board's stated doubt; the closing act asks for it. Not drawn — “Cannot delete” when policy rules refuse Wertpapiere → Löschen · H8.2 (#918)

The design critic saw a refusal by policy rules name only the rules, not the security's bookings and quotes. The spec decides this case and keeps it: DESIGN.md → H8.2 counts what blocks “from the refusal's {:referenced, counts}”, and states that “the policy-rule state (the rule list) is unchanged but for its name, now in <bdi>”, because rules are refused before anything is counted (ADR-0049 §8). Board 08 draws the research-entry and the merge states only. Counting bookings and quotes beside the rules would be a new decision about the refusal's payload, not a repair; it is reported back, not built here.
