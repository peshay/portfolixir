B1 — The coupon and the price read as stored Wertpapier → Übersicht → Anleihe · Kupon, Laufende Rendite

The coupon is an input and the price the yield used is a stored quote, but both went through Format.decimal(_, 2): a coupon of 4,125 % read “4,13 %”, and its ratio line “4,13 ÷ 97,13” for a quote of 97,125 — two figures the statement does not carry, with a current yield computed from the unrounded ones beside them. After: the coupon and the price in the ratio line are shown as stored, trailing zeros trimmed (Format.exact, as the quantity, the denomination and the two-scales note already are); the computed figures keep their two places. A coupon of 2,5 % reads “2,5 %”, as the dialog shows it.

B2 — No yield from a trade price on the unit scale the same block, a bond booked at its nominal with no quote yet

10.000 Stück bought at 0,984 — the export booked the nominal as the quantity — and no quote stored. The two-scales guard needs a quote, so it is silent, and the yields fall back to the last own trade price: “2,50 ÷ 0,98” is a current yield of 254 % and the linear yield to maturity reads about 2.400 %. After: when the yields would use the own trade price and that price is at most 5 — the mirror of the guard's band, 100 ÷ 20 — they are not computed. Each cell reads nicht berechenbar with the reason under it, in the tone of the other reasons, and the API says the same (price_on_unit_scale: true, stated in the computation basis). The nominal stays as stored: the block shows what was booked. A quote, once stored, is a percent price, the yields compute from it, and the guard names the two scales.

B3 — An inbound delivery's booked price is read by the guard Wertpapier → Übersicht, the two-scales note · Vermögen → Bestände → Datenqualität

Since #779 a priced inbound delivery opens a lot and drives the day's flow at its booked price, exactly as a buy does, but the guard read buys only: a bond delivered in at 0,985 per unit and quoted 97,25 stood at a nominal of 1.000.000 with no note at all. After: the guard reads every booked price per unit — a buy's and a priced inbound delivery's — and the note counts “Buchungen”, not “Käufe”. The rule in the payload says “a booked price per unit (a buy or a priced inbound delivery)”, and its keys become unit_scale_bookings and last_unit_scale_booking. The Wealth note asks for the quantity of the bond's bookings.

B4 — “Anleihedaten erfassen…” is a 44 px target on touch H6.1 · UX-DR6 · the bond block's empty state

The empty state's remedy is a .link-button inside its sentence, as a data note's remedy is, but the sentence is a p.detail-tab-empty, not a .data-note__body, so H6.1's coarse rule never reached it: 18 px tall under a touch pointer. After: the rule takes this remedy as one more selector, scoped to the bond block's empty state — the same 13 px of block padding and the same 13 px given back, so the sentence keeps its 18 px line and nothing reflows. Desktop with a mouse is unchanged. Dashed red/green = the hit box as the browser lays it out (drawing aid); the After frame applies the coarse declarations without the media query, because the renderer has a mouse.
