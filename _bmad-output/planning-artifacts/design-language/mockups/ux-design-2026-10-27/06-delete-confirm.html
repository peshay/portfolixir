① Q6 · H17 — Delete asks whatever the menu saw when it opened „Aktionen für Tagesgeld“ · account_menu/1

Today the menu reads what references the account as it opens (handle_event("open_account_menu", …)), and the Delete item carries data-confirm={if @menu.refs == %{}, do: delete_confirmation(…)}: a free account asks once (board 01 of the lifecycle pass, G1-A), a referenced one opens the refusal „Kann nicht gelöscht werden“ directly. The snapshot is taken when the menu opens. When the references go between that moment and the click — the bookings merged away in another tab, or by the agent — the item has no confirmation, and delete_account/3 finds the account free and deletes it with no prompt and no word on the page. The destructive-confirmations invariant does not catch it: it finds the text data-confirm= on the element, which the conditional satisfies.

Variante A empfohlen — the item always asks; the sentence follows what the menu saw

A — das Menü sah Buchungen: die Rückfrage behauptet nichts
A — das Menü sah ein freies Konto: die Rückfrage wie heute

Why A.

  • The rule becomes literal: the Delete item carries data-confirm unconditionally, so the invariant can assert the attribute's value is never empty, and no snapshot can switch the ask off.
  • The sentence stays true. A free account keeps today's („… Das Konto hat keine Buchungen und kein verknüpftes Depot.“). A menu that saw references asks without claiming emptiness and says what happens if they are still there: OK → still referenced → the refusal opens as today; no longer referenced → deleted, as asked.
  • The cost is one prompt before a refusal the operator usually expects. G1-A's „a referenced account opens its refusal directly“ becomes „… after one ask“; DESIGN.md records it.

Variante B — the server refuses on a stale menu and asks to choose again

B — nichts gelöscht; das Ergebnis bittet um eine neue Wahl

B keeps G1-A as it is: the item carries the snapshot it was opened with; a delete sent from a menu that saw references never deletes. Still referenced → the refusal; free now → nothing is deleted and the page's result says so; the next menu opens free and asks once. Against it: two menus and two clicks to delete what is now free; a result note for a state the operator did not cause; and the invariant has to read the event's handling, not the element, to stay meaningful.
