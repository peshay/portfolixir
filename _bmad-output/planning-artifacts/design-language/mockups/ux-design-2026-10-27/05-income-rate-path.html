① Q5 · K07 — a payment with no rate path to EUR „Erträge“ · Portfolixir.Portfolios.Income convert/4

The case. „Velden Water AG“ paid a dividend of 52,00 CHF into a CHF account on 08.01.2026. The instance stores CHF rates from its daily sync since 12.01.2026 and has never run the history backfill, so no CHF rate exists on or before the booking date. Today Income.convert/4 falls back to parity when Fx.convert/4 finds no path: the 52,00 CHF are added to the EUR totals as 52,00 EUR, and the entry is counted in unconverted_count, which the JSON API serialises and no page reads. The facet’s ⓘ says „Beträge über den EUR-Hub zum gespeicherten Kurs des jeweiligen Buchungsdatums … umgerechnet“ — for this payment, untrue. Every other facet of the page (Realisiert, Ein- & Auszahlungen, Kosten) excludes such an amount and names it.

Heute — 52,00 CHF als 52,00 EUR in 2026 und in den Top-Beiträgen; kein Wort
Heute — 390 px

A sum of two currencies, read as one. The 2026 bar and its label (412,85) include 52,00 that are francs; the top contributors rank Velden Water by francs among euros. A franc moves against the euro by a few per cent a year, so the error is small here — and unbounded for a currency far from parity.

Variante A empfohlen — out of every total, named, with the backfill as its remedy

A — die Notiz vor den Zahlen; 2026 ohne die 52,00; Velden Water nicht unter den Top-Beiträgen
A — 390 px

Why A.

  • It is the page's own rule. Realisiert, Ein- & Auszahlungen and Kosten each exclude an amount they cannot convert and name it in an attention note that leads the facet („… and is excluded from every total“), never at a neighbouring date's rate; Sprint 21's α3 extends the same shape to an unavailable trade (board 09, N8). Income is the one facet that adds the unconverted amount instead.
  • The remedy exists and sits in the note: fx_backfill_control/1, as on Realisiert. After a backfill the payment converts at its own day's rate and the note disappears.
  • The note names count and currencies (the fix) and, like the realized note, the securities, so the operator finds the payment.
  • Every figure of the facet agrees: the bars, the year labels, the matrix, the top contributors and the per-position table all leave the payment out; the payments table of the drilled year keeps the payment in its own currency, as it lists native amounts.

Variante B — parity stays; the basis line says so

B — „1 Zahlung in CHF ohne Wechselkurs, 1 : 1 gezählt“
B — 390 px

B changes no figure and states the basis in the facet's basis line and its ⓘ. Against it: it keeps a sum of two currencies and asks the reader to discount it; it is the only facet that would do so; and a basis line is the weakest place for a fact that makes a total wrong (UX-DR17 puts such a fact in an attention note). B fits only if the owner wants Income's totals never to drop a payment.
