Why A.

  • It is the one money consequence the merge has. Holdings are conserved and checked; the realized figures are not, and closed years change. They belong in view before the confirm, next to the holdings identity they complement — not behind a click.
  • No new anatomy. The depot preview already states before and after per position in .merge-positions (a table above 720 px, .merge-lines below) with a basis line; the block reuses it: per close year „Quelle · Ziel → Danach“ and the change, then the open lots' cost.
  • Unchanged says so in one line. A pair with no sale in either queue, or whose matching does not move, shows „Realisierte Gewinne (FIFO) ändern sich nicht“ in place of the table, so the block's absence never has to be read.
  • The digest covers it (the fix): a changed plan whose restatement moved answers with the fresh preview, as for holdings.
  • Board 09 (2026-10-09) stands: a closed trade α3 makes unavailable is not summed; the block's basis line names it, the facet's sentence.
