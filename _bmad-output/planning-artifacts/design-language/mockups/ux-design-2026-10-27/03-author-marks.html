Q3 — Variante A empfohlen — „(Agent)“ after the institution in the basis line

A — „Bank B (Agent), Bank C (Agent)“; Köpfe und Aufträge mit „· Agent“
A — 390 px

Why A. The mark stands next to what it qualifies, as on Risk: in a comma list a closing „· Agent“ would read as a mark on the last institution or on the whole line, so the word goes in parentheses after the institution it covers. One word per institution; the hidden lead („Abrechnung von:“) tells a reader what it is. B repeats the institution's name in a second clause and grows the line by a clause for each institution, and the reader has to match the two lists.
