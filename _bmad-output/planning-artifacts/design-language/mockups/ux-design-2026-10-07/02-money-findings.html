A — empfohlen

A — empfohlen, 390 px

Why A. UX-DR17 gives problem to a finding where "the data contradicts itself; the figure cannot be trusted", attention to one where "the figure stands". This predicate is a contradiction by construction: two stored facts about the same security on the same day, the operator's booked price and the quote, more than a factor of two apart. One of them is wrong, and either way a figure in the totals is — the value, when the quote is wrong (a mis-mapped ticker, a currency unit), or the cost and result, when the booking is (a mistyped price). The band is wide on purpose: a real trade at less than half or more than twice its day's market is rare enough that the finding is almost never an opinion.

The sibling says the same. The two-scales guard (#1068, D-15) is this predicate's special case — ratio ~100 on a bond — and it is problem on Wealth and on the Overview's line. A tenfold error a step outside its band would be quieter than a hundredfold one inside it only because of which rule caught it. Under A the Overview's line takes "Problem" (UX-DR17: the highest severity present), as it does for two scales.

The words. The line: "2 gehaltene Wertpapiere, deren Kurse nicht zu ihren Buchungen passen" — its scope word ("gehaltene"), because the line otherwise counts the catalog (J1.2), and always its noun, as two scales. Wealth: the consequence ("ihr Wert oder ihr Einstand ist daher falsch") and the remedy in order — the mapping first, then the booking — and per security one booking: kind, date, price, the quote's own date (Arbolia's is the day before: no quote stored on 03.04.), the quote, the ratio. Each name links to its Quotes tab. The list: a removable chip "Kurs passt nicht zu Buchungen", as two_scales' "Auf zwei Skalen bepreist".

B reads the predicate as a heuristic: an off-market booking — employee shares at a discount, a subscription price, a gift booked at a nominal value — could trip it, so the figure "stands" until the operator looks. The note then sits with the stale-quote note, says "kann … falsch sein", and the Overview's line keeps "Achtung".

What B costs. On the Overview a holding ten times off reads exactly like "25 Wertpapiere im Katalog ohne Kurs seit 7 Tagen" — same colour, glyph and word — while a bond a hundred times off, one line along, is "Problem". The false positives B protects against are rare at [½, 2] (a discount under 50 %, a premium over 100 %), and the most common one, a free share booked at 0,00, has no ratio at all and must be skipped either way (found while drawing, 2). A false positive under A costs one look at a booking; a true positive under B is a total off by a factor of ten, announced as a stale quote.
