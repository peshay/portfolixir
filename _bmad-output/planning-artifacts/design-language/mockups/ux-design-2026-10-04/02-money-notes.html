B. "The Wealth note" in #1055 could be read as this one, and it already talks about cash without a rate. It is the wrong home. The data-quality section reports today's state and has no period, while the jump belongs to a period: under "Max" or "1J" it is in the result, under "YTD 2027" it is not, and this note cannot say which.
