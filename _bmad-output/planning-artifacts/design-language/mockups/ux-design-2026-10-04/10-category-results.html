J10 — B The plan editor's select becomes a page-local scope select one select, this page only

Argument and costs. B reuses what the screen has: the editor's select moves out of the editor to the top of the page and now steers both the plan and the tree, without a navigation and without touching the cross-page view. That is its whole advantage — a change of scope on an administration screen does not change what Wealth shows next. Its costs: (1) a second form of scope control in an app that has one — a select where every other scoped surface has chips (UX-DR16 class 2 for filters; D4 removed exactly this „Ansicht“ label from the switcher); (2) two vocabularies — the editor's „Gesamt“ and „Sicht“ against „Alles“ and „Ansicht“ everywhere else, which B either keeps or must rename anyway; (3) two answers to „which view is active“: arriving from Wealth on „Langfrist“, the screen opens on „Gesamt“, and the operator reads a different scope's result without having chosen it; (4) no plan dots, unless the select grows them as option text. A gives the same single scope with the control and the words the operator already knows.

J10.2 · #1048 — A recommended „Alles“ in EUR; a member whose cost was not paid in EUR is named, not added view „Alles“ chosen · drawn on A's screen

Argument. #1048 asks the screen to „state one currency the same way“ as the #901 read, which already decided it: a view's roll-up is in EUR, and a member held in a portfolio whose base currency is not EUR has no EUR cost to add, so it is excluded and named (CategoryResult.for_view/3, in_hub_currency/2). The screen follows that rule for every scope, „Alles“ included, so the screen and the agent's read cannot disagree. ADR-0041 §4 is the shape (excluded and named, never counted as zero, the row stating how many it covers) and UX-DR25 the appearance: one attention note where the totals are read, with the count, the name and the native figure (clause 2: never a converted one), and no button (clause 3: there is nothing to fix). Because „Wert“ still includes the member, the note says so (UX-DR26: a stated difference a reader could otherwise reconcile by subtracting). Not drawn — B, convert through the EUR hub: a cost is what was paid (ADR-0033); converting 1.500,00 USD at today's rate states a cost nobody paid, converting at each purchase date's rate is a new derived metric with its own computation basis, and either way the screen would then disagree with the API's view read, which excludes. A per-currency subtotal (two figures per cell) and blanking the whole category's cost for one USD member were also weighed and are worse: the first breaks the column grid of C8-A, the second hides five members to avoid naming one.
