## Decisions taken during this run

- 2026-08-12 — Workspace created; `brief.md` skeleton on disk with
  `status: draft`. Fast path chosen (see above).
- 2026-08-12 — **Operator-side success criteria stay qualitative.** The owner
  states plainly that they have no measurements; the felt signal arrives through
  the chat (stale figures, visible tool-grinding, token burn). Recorded as such
  rather than converted into invented KPIs. Two cheap observable proxies are
  offered in the brief and explicitly tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` — proposed by the
  PM, not requested by the owner. Consequence: the agent-side criteria carry the
  measurable weight of this brief, which is honest, because that is where the
  operator experiences the system.
- 2026-08-12 — **Audience question resolved; the ambiguity was the PM's.** The
  question "is adoption by other self-hosters a goal or a side effect" was read
  as "would someone host this for you". Owner's answer settles it either way:
  open source, everyone hosts their own instance with their own data and their
  own model. Recorded as the deployment model, not a growth target — and it is
  the reason the README must make the value legible quickly. No positioning
  section beyond the honest comparison already in the brief.
- 2026-08-12 — **Structural framing added to the problem statement** (PM
  contribution, open to correction): the "nothing derived is ever stored" rule
  was read as "nothing derived may ever be kept", which is stricter than
  auditability requires and is the shared cause of both the operator's waiting
  and the agent's recomputation. This is the sentence the derived-value ADR has
  to answer.
- 2026-08-12 — `addendum.md` created for downstream depth: the `AGENTS.md`
  amendments (Project Goal, two-way coverage rule, maintenance lane), the
  derived-layer invariants, the local-model ordering, README story scope, the
  parked items with reasons, and the evidence-base note.

- 2026-08-12 — **Editorial passes run on `brief.md`** (structure and prose, per
  `doc_standards`). Applied: the count mismatches fixed (five drift cases were
  promised and three delivered; two working rules were announced as one
  sentence), Scope moved ahead of audience and positioning per pyramid order,
  the executive summary's third paragraph folded into one sentence, the
  operator-side justification de-duplicated against the problem statement, a
  gate header added naming what the brief decides and which artifacts change,
  the vague response-size figure replaced with the measured one, front-end
  jargon removed from the summary, and several hedges cut.
  **Two findings rejected, deliberately:**
  1. *"is thinner here than it should be" → "is largely missing here"* — this
     would be **false**. Closed trades are computed and exposed over the API and
     in the securities detail pane; TTWROR, IRR and MWR all exist. What is
     missing is a surface to read them on, and the text now says that instead.
  2. *"will stay ahead for a while" → "will stay ahead"* — dropping the bound
     asserts a permanent deficit against Portfolio Performance, which is the
     opposite of the plan.
