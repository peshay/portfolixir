# UX review — 2026-09-12: the whole surface, walked

Role: UX designer (the standing design authority of ADR-0038), on the owner's
ask of 2026-09-12: *look at the whole UI/UX, find what can and should be
improved, file issues with mockups where several good solutions exist, and
collect enough for a UI/UX sprint.* This document is the authoritative
spec pointer for every issue it filed; the issues are thin (issue-tracking
convention), the criteria live here and in the two spines.

**Status: written as adopted — the merge of the planning PR that carries it
signs the designer's decisions in Part 4 (ADR-0026 step 1 as amended by
PR #780). Variant choices (Part 3) are the owner's and are made by one comment
on the PR or on the issue; the recommended variant is listed first and ships
if no comment says otherwise.**

## How the review ran (so the claim is checkable)

Conditions per `review-rubric.md` / #706 — the ones the 2026-08-15 triage found
missing from every previous pass:

- **DE locale** on every surface; EN desktop shots taken as the comparison.
- **1440 px and 390 px**, light and dark, full-page, through Playwright
  (device emulation, true CSS pixels), against a development server running
  the tree at `1e78853` with the synthetic `priv/demo/` dataset.
- **Seed data that fires every alarm surface**: on top of the demo export the
  review seeded a held position whose quote feed stopped five weeks ago, an
  inbound delivery with no quote and no asset class, a watch-list security,
  a USD cash account with a balance and no FX rate, a snapshot 90 days back,
  a tax profile plus a recorded prior-year statement, a research log with a
  thesis, evidence, a risk and the retraction that supersedes it, two buckets
  and one view. All synthetic; the script is kept as provenance next to the
  mockups (`design-language/mockups/ux-review-2026-09-12/seed-finding-surfaces.exs`).
- **Interaction states**, not only routes: every securities detail tab, the
  new-security dialog, the row menu, the column picker, the filter popover,
  the custom range, the account dialog, the bucket picker, the import preview
  and result, the classification tree, the phone sidebar.

Roughly one hundred shots were taken; the twenty-eight that carry a finding
are committed as `before-*.png` beside the mockups. Everything in this document
was verified in the tree (`lib/portfolixir_web/`, `priv/static/app.css`,
`priv/gettext/de/`) after it was seen on screen — a finding that was only
seen is marked as such.

Dedup basis: the open-issue list (27 open before this review filed anything),
the closed issues since 2026-08-12 (#700–#732, #740–#752, #757–#777), and the

## Part 0 — three findings that govern the rest

**0.1 — The spec is ahead of the build on four surfaces, and the gap is now
the most visible drift.** Tax, Snapshots, Views and the Wealth data-quality
list each have a paragraph in `EXPERIENCE.md` → Component Patterns that says
what they are (a budget meter plus a check list; the comparison as the
surface; read-first rows; data notes with the remedy inside). The build has
none of the four. The rules were written on 2026-08-05; the alignment stories
were "cut from the spec" but never cut. Part 2 cuts them.

**0.2 — "Editing zero intents away" is the app's most repeated habit.** The
Transactions page *is* its form; the securities detail pane opens on an edit
form; Tax opens on a thirteen-field form; Views has two inline forms above
its two lists; Accounts puts four bucket controls in one cell. The spine's
progressive-disclosure rule ("creating and editing are one intent away, never
zero") is stated once and broken on five surfaces. Part 3's largest proposals
are all this one rule applied.

**0.3 — The phone is a scaled desktop.** At 390 px the Wealth KPI band is
840 px of stacked cards before any content; the securities list is a wide
table of which only the name column is visible; the drift table clips its
value columns instead of scrolling; the filter chrome is three rows of chips.
The responsive work so far kept things *reachable* (D6, #702, #730). This
review proposes the layouts that make them *readable*.

## Part 3 — Composition proposals, with variants

Where two or three good answers exist the mockup shows them side by side.
Recommended variant first; the reason in one sentence.

**C1 — Wealth KPI band.** Seven cards in a 5+2 grid; "12.473,00 EUR · 25,1 %"
and "43.560,75 · +1.500,00 EUR" wrap to three lines; the band repeats on the
Allocation tab; on the phone it is 840 px of cards. `01-wealth-kpi-band--1200.png`
/ `--390.png`.
- **A — two tiers (recommended):** three lead figures (total, TTWROR, IRR) at
  full size, four supporting figures at half height; currency as a small
  suffix so the number never wraps. Reads as a hierarchy, which is what
  "seven equal cards" lacks.
- **B — one strip:** seven cells in one row, swipeable on the phone. Densest;
  loses the card identity the owner likes.
- **C — 4+3 grid, 24 px values:** the conservative fix.
- In all variants the Allocation tab does not repeat the band; it carries one
  summary line (total · cash quote · TTWROR) linking back to Holdings.

**C2 — Overview.** Sixty percent of a 1440×900 viewport is empty; the value
card has no trend; the drift rows have no visual and no visible link
affordance. `02-overview.png`.
- **A — sparkline in the value card, drift bars in the rows (recommended):**
  stays inside UX-DR2 as rewritten (value + change, off-target, data quality)

## Part 4 — Decided here (designer's call, owner-delegated per ADR-0038 §3)

Recorded as rules so the issues can point at them; the same text is appended
to the spines as the 2026-09-12 amendment.

1. **Under 560 px a wide list renders as two-line rows, not as a horizontally
   scrolling table** (new rule, UX-DR27). Name over identifiers; the figures
   the row exists for on the right; row states (stale, unclassified, no price)
   as words in the row. Tables that are matrices (year × month) keep the
   scroller of UX-DR15; lists of entities do not scroll sideways.
2. **A facet's page subtitle names the facet**, not the parent's first facet
   (extends UX-DR21). Realized gains reads "Realisierte Gewinne und Verluste
   aus Verkäufen", Flows "Ein- und Auszahlungen", Costs "Gebühren und
   Steuern".
3. **A Cash-flow facet states its composition in one basis line**: amounts
   currency · what is aggregated · scope. Exclusions and method go into one ⓘ
   on that line. No facet opens with a paragraph.
4. **A KPI band renders on one tab.** A sibling tab of the same area carries a
   one-line summary linking to the band, never the band again.
5. **A value never wraps inside its slot.** The currency is a small suffix of
   the value; a combined figure ("cash · cash quote") is a value plus a
   sub-line, not two values in one `<strong>`. Extends {components.value-slot}.
6. **Zero cells in a matrix render as a quiet "–"** in {colors.text-subtle},
   so the non-zero cells are what the eye finds. Applies to the income matrix
   and the realized matrix.
7. **A raw enum value never reaches a user-facing string.** Every kind, feed,
   source and status label goes through gettext; a fallback clause that prints
   the value is a defect. Ask for the story: a meta-test over
   `Transaction.kinds/0` against `tx_type_label/1`.
8. **A stale quote is marked where the price is read**: attention tone, the
   clock glyph (added to the icon set with this story), the word "veraltet"
   and the quote date — in the list row, the detail header and the holdings
   valuation (State Patterns → Stale data, now with its call sites).
9. **The sunburst's centre carries the touched segment** (name, value, actual
   vs target), and the plan's Σ line becomes the chart's basis line.
10. **Every control row sits inside the content grid.** The view switcher and
    the facet control take the section's horizontal padding; nothing is
    flush with the sidebar edge or the screen edge.
11. **A destructive row action lives in the row menu**, never as a standing
    button on every row (Snapshots "Löschen", Tax "Löschen").
12. **The top-bar title's line box clears its diacritics**: `line-height`
    ≥ 1.3 with `overflow-x: clip` on the container, not `overflow: hidden` on
    the heading.
