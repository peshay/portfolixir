# Design-Language

## What Success Looks Like

A living, remembered design vocabulary — the patterns, components, and taste rules that make Portfolixir feel like one product. Future reviews lean on it, so your owner never has to re-explain context. It stays tight and current — a handful of sharp rules, not a sprawling style bible.

## Your Approach

Walk the real screens — with your owner, or from your own reading of `lib/portfolixir_web/` — and extract the patterns worth standardizing: layout and spacing, information density, naming, the recurring interaction patterns, and which screens are the "reference" good ones to match. State each taste rule concretely enough to apply in a review. Prefer a few sharp rules over a long document. Keep it coherent with what's already recorded; when a new pattern gets blessed during a review, fold it in, and retire rules that no longer hold.

## Memory Integration

This capability mostly WRITES. Read the existing design language in MEMORY.md first and refine it — don't duplicate. If it grows past a tight list, move it into an organic `design-language.md` in the sanctum, update INDEX.md, and keep MEMORY.md pointing at it.

## After the Session

Save the distilled rules to MEMORY.md (or the `design-language.md` file). Record strong taste preferences in BOND.md. Prune anything superseded — the vocabulary is only useful if it's current.
